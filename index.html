Love is a beautiful, catastrophic madness. It is the willing surrender of your own soul to a beautiful tyrant, handing them a polished blade and trusting them never to cut you, even as they press it against your throat. It is an infinite, burning galaxy born within the cramped cage of a human chest—a fire so violent and consuming that it turns every past memory to ash, yet so soft that it can heal a broken mind with a single, unsaid word. To love is to blind yourself to the gravity of the earth, choosing instead to fall endlessly into another person’s orbit, charting the maps of your entire existence by the constellation of their eyes. It is a holy, terrifying alchemy that transforms ordinary, fleeting heartbeats into a timeless, immortal symphony. It is the only force in the universe that can simultaneously make you a king in a golden palace and a beggar weeping in the ruins—and make you thank the skies for both.
